About

Welcome to my portfolio! I’m Aidan—an audio generalist with 5 years of experience and a passion for sound design, game audio, recording, mixing, editing, and working on cool projects. My mission is to create soundscapes that enhance experiences and drive others to create.

You can find my recent work as well as my toolkit below. If you like what you see/hear, feel free to contact me for inquiries!

Anatomy of the Terrible

Anatomy of the Terrible is an ongoing project built from scratch by three audio engineers: Kyle Asman, Trevor Brown, and Aidan Reed. It is a small-scale horror experience focused on exploration and puzzle solving in an ever intensifying environment. Enter the home, explore its secrets, do what you can to get out alive.
